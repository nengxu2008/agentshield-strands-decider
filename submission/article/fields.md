AgentShield: Strands Decider 2B as the first-pass gatekeeper that routes security checks for third-party AI agents

Description (max 512 characters):
AgentShield guards the steps third-party AI agents take. Strands Decider 2B makes one batched decision per step: fast allow, fast block, a targeted trusted lookup, a deeper Amazon Bedrock analyzer, or both. Hard policy rules always have the final word. On held-out cases it matched Amazon Nova Pro's 61.1% accuracy at $0.0350 instead of $0.6003 per 1,000 decisions, and was always right when confident.
